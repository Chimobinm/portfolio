Hello, I'm Francis Obinna Madubuike

Dr. Obinna C. Madubuike's research focuses on digital twin technology in construction and healthcare facility management — examining how virtual representations of physical assets improve monitoring, operation, and maintenance of buildings and infrastructure.

His research highlights the limitations of traditional facility management approaches, which often rely on reactive maintenance, and proposes digital twin–based frameworks integrating BIM, IoT sensors, and data analytics for real-time monitoring, predictive maintenance, and better lifecycle decision-making. His work also includes systematic and bibliometric reviews of digital twin applications, identifying research trends, benefits, and gaps for deploying these systems in healthcare and other constructed assets.
